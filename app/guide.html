Sign in and act on a story

An agent wrote this guide. It is a draft. A person adopts it.

This guide tells you how to sign in, find a story, claim it, and close it.

1. Sign in with a passkey

Use this way if you joined in this browser with a passkey.

  1. Open roots.coop/app in the browser where you joined.
  2. The app shows Welcome back and your name.
  3. Push Sign in with passkey.
  4. Your device asks for your face, your fingerprint or your screen lock. Give it.
  5. The app shows Signed in as and your name.

A passkey opens only the account that this browser keeps. If your key is not in this browser, use part 2.

2. Sign in with a remote signer

Use this way if a remote signer app (NIP-46) holds your key. The steward signs in on the phone this way.

  1. Open your remote signer app. Copy its connection address. The address starts with bunker://.
  2. Open roots.coop/app.
  3. Push Other ways to sign in.
  4. Paste the address into the field that shows bunker://....
  5. Push Connect a remote signer (NIP-46).
  6. The app shows Waiting for the remote signer to approve.... Approve the connection in your signer app.
  7. The app shows Signed in as and the start of your key.

Your signer app may ask you to approve each event. Approve only the event that the app showed you.

Never type your secret key into this app. Do not send the connection address to another person.

No automatic test signs in this way. A person checks it on a phone.

3. Find a story

  1. Push the tab Coordination.
  2. The board shows one column for each state of a story.
  3. Find the column Ready. A ready story waits for a claim.
  4. To see fewer stories, use Show the stories of and choose a team.
  5. Each card shows the id, the name and the state of a story. A card can also show Gate: person only.
  6. The app shows Claim this story only on a ready story under a function that you hold.

4. Claim a story

  1. Push Claim this story on the card.
  2. A dialog shows each line of the event before you sign it. Read the lines.
  3. To stop, push Cancel. The app signs nothing.
  4. To sign, push Sign and send.
  5. The card moves to the column Claimed. The card shows Claimed by and your name.

You hold one open claim at a time. The app offers no other claim until this claim closes.

To release a claim, push Release my claim. Type the reason in Reason. Then push Sign and send.

5. Post a proof

A proof names a file of your work in the corp-agi repository. Put the file there first.

  1. Push Post a proof on the card in the column Claimed.
  2. The dialog tells you what the proof answers. For a first proof, it shows This proof answers the claim.
  3. The option In the corp-agi repository is selected. Keep it.
  4. In File path in corp-agi, type the path of the file. An example is notes/plan-6/D-01.3.md.
  5. In The file, choose the same file. The browser calculates its sha256. The file stays on your device.
  6. If the file is not on this device, open Type the sha256 instead. Type the sha256 of the file.
  7. In Review by the function, choose the function that reviews the proof.
  8. Push Sign and send.
  9. The card moves to the column In review.

If a field is not correct, the app signs nothing. It shows Correct and the number of fields. Correct them, then push Sign and send again.

6. Wait for the review

An agent reads your proof and signs a review. The agent does this outside the app.

  • If the review passes on a person-only story, the card moves to the column Done, waits for a person.
  • If the review fails, the card goes back to Claimed. The dialog of your next proof shows the defects.
  • An agent never closes a person-only story. The history of the card shows the done of an agent as Not accepted.

7. Close a person-only story

Only the custodian of the program signs this done.

  1. Find the card in the column Done, waits for a person.
  2. Push Sign the done.
  3. The dialog shows each line of the event. Read the lines.
  4. Push Sign and send.
  5. The card moves to the column Done. It shows Done, signed by, your name and [steward].

The app offers a control. The lifecycle rules decide what a signed event does. The board shows the result.
